Vessel Overview

The Torpedo Ray is the primary vessel used for Aqualux excursions. It is maintained through scheduled inspections, daily operational checks, and preventive maintenance to ensure passenger safety, reliability, and premium guest experiences.

Daily Responsibilities

  • Complete pre-departure safety inspections.
  • Verify all emergency equipment.
  • Confirm inventory and fuel levels.
  • Inspect engines and navigation systems.
  • Report maintenance issues immediately.

Daily Workflow

Daily Operations

Complete each operational task before and after every excursion to maintain Aqualux's standards of safety and service.

Departure Readiness

Verify passenger safety equipment, navigation systems, weather conditions, fuel levels, and departure clearance.

Maintenance Tracking

Record inspections, preventative maintenance, repairs, and any equipment requiring attention.

Inventory Control

Monitor refreshments, cleaning supplies, safety equipment, and onboard inventory.
